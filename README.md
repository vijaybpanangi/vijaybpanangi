### 🧱 What I do

I started as a software engineer in India after studying Computer Science at **Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University**, and over the past **19 years** I've worked my way through five countries and a career arc that took me from writing code to leading the teams that turn data into decisions. That journey has gone through Hyderabad, Atlanta, New York, London, Kuwait City, and now Toronto, across industries from energy and capital banking to consumer retail, micro-finance, and loyalty analytics.

Today I'm the **Manager of BI & Analytics** at **Exchange Solutions** in Toronto, where I lead the team that builds the dashboards, cubes, and analytical data products powering promotional analytics for retail loyalty programs. Snowflake is my warehouse, Tableau is my canvas, and SQL is the language I think in when I'm solving a business question. I've also built an **end-to-end AI-assisted BI delivery pipeline** using Claude, spanning everything from discovery and requirements through architecture, development, validation, and knowledge transfer.

But the thing I love most about this work isn't any single tool. It's the moment when a stakeholder looks at a dashboard and says, *"Oh, now I see it."* That's the puzzle I keep coming back to: how do you take something complex and make it genuinely clear?

When the workday wraps, I don't stop building. I've shipped **four live web products** on Cloudflare's edge, each one born from something I actually cared about. I care about shipping things completely: not just the application code, but the DNS records, the email infrastructure, the SEO, the payment processing, the monitoring. If it's part of the product, I want to understand how it works.

### 🚀 What I've built

| Project | What it is | Stack |
|---|---|---|
| ✍️ [**A Wonderful Life**](https://awonderfullife.ca) | My personal essay blog where I write about data, life, and the space between. Started as hand-edited HTML (update four files per post), rebuilt on **Eleventy v3** so a new essay is a single Markdown file and everything generates. Dark mode with no-FOUC pre-paint, Pagefind static search, a Turnstile-protected newsletter backed by D1, and an appreciation button with a salted per-IP rate-limited reactions API on its own Worker. Deep SEO: `Person.sameAs`, `BreadcrumbList`, real `og:image` dimensions measured from each JPEG at build. 18+ essays across Reflection, Society, Politics, Technology, and Travel. | Eleventy · Workers · D1 · Turnstile · Pagefind |
| 🇨🇦 [**Canadian To Be**](https://canadiantobe.ca) | A satirical "Ministry of Apologies" site born from the Canada-US trade tensions. What started as a single-page joke grew through **13 shipped modules** into a real product: a citizenship quiz with five personality archetypes and shareable SVG certificates (embedded bilingual fonts, stable URLs, OG meta for social cards), a sticker store processing real payments through **Stripe Checkout** with webhook-driven fulfillment and a Buy More & Save bulk pricing engine, promo codes via **Cloudflare Cron Triggers** (a genuine workaround for Stripe having no native delayed-start field), **D3-rendered maps** of Canada and the world, live weather data, 74 watermarked designs across 12 curated packs, a full Housekeeping analytics dashboard, and a broadsheet-style redesign that ties the whole thing together. **530+ tests**, TypeScript strict, full SEO suite. | Workers · D1 · Stripe · TypeScript · D3.js · Cron Triggers |
| 🎓 [**Ezzi Clarity**](https://ezziclarity.ca) | Trilingual marketing site (English, French, Arabic with full RTL) for a family educational consulting practice. This site has had **four distinct lives**: an English-only stand-in, a WordPress "Liquid Glass" theme with backdrop-blur everywhere, a refined WP theme with Plus Jakarta Sans and a Sky/Peach/Cream palette, and finally a complete rebuild as **pure static HTML and one CSS file** on Cloudflare Pages. No build system, no JS framework, no dependencies. Frosted-glass design with `--glass*` tokens, **Fraunces italic** for Latin serif accents, **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** for Arabic headings, IntersectionObserver scroll reveals gated by `prefers-reduced-motion`, iCloud Custom Email with SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and a books page being wired to a 7-title children's book catalogue on Amazon KDP. | HTML · CSS · Cloudflare Pages · iCloud Email |
| 📋 [**Track Your App**](https://trackyourapp.ca) | An immigration tracker I built because I went through the Canadian Express Entry process myself and wished something better existed. CRS score draw history, processing time trend analysis broken down by stage, interactive guides (eligibility, BOWP, SOWP, citizenship, CRS calculator, glossary), and an insights section that surfaces patterns most applicants miss. Built a **Civic Record design system** from scratch for this project: five meaning-color families (maple/blue/green/amber/violet), each with consistent token layers, being migrated progressively across routes. **Seven locales**, thousands of daily users. Running as a Next.js app on Cloudflare Workers via OpenNext. This is the project I'm most proud of because it helps people during one of the most stressful waits of their lives. | Next.js · Workers · TypeScript · i18n · OpenNext |
