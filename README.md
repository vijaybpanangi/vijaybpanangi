### 🧱 What I do

I've spent **19 years** at the intersection of business strategy and data, across five countries and six cities. That journey has taken me from regulated records management and capital banking at **GE** in London and New York, to enterprise analytics across consumer retail, food and beverage, and micro-finance at **Alghanim Industries** in Kuwait City, to loyalty analytics here in Toronto. Every chapter has reinforced the same conviction: great analytics starts with the business question, not the technology. The tools are a gift; what you do with them for the people making decisions is the point.

Today I'm the **Manager of BI & Analytics** at **Exchange Solutions**, where I lead the team that translates raw transactional data into governed dashboards, cubes, and analytical products powering promotional strategy for retail loyalty programs. I care about the whole arc: partnering with stakeholders to understand what they actually need to know, building data products that make answers clear and trustworthy, driving adoption so the work actually changes how people decide, and strengthening governance so the numbers stay reliable. I've also designed an **end-to-end AI-assisted BI delivery pipeline** using Claude, because it genuinely accelerates the path from business question to validated, governed insight.

> *The thing I love most about this work isn't any single tool. It's the moment when a stakeholder looks at a dashboard and says, "Oh, now I see it." That's the puzzle I keep coming back to: how do you take something complex and make it genuinely clear?*

I studied Computer Science and Engineering at **Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University** in India, and I've carried that technical foundation through every role since. When the workday wraps, I keep building. I've shipped **four live web products** on Cloudflare's edge, each one born from a real problem I wanted to solve. I think the best way to truly understand how a business works is to own every layer of the product yourself: the application code, the DNS, the email infrastructure, the SEO, the payment processing, the monitoring.
